SimpleEqualizer

Volume control and a 10-band equalizer for Mac outputs whose volume macOS can't change.

Download View on GitHub

macOS 27 or later · Mac with Apple silicon · Free (MIT License)

What it does

When you send sound from your Mac to speakers through an optical digital output (USB SPDIF Adapter) or similar, macOS can't change the volume, and the speakers play at full volume. SimpleEqualizer is a menu bar app that controls the volume in the app itself. It also adds an equalizer.

  • Volume and muteChange the volume from the menu bar popup. It is remembered for each output.
  • Volume keysUse the keyboard volume keys (up, down, mute) as usual.
  • 10-band equalizer31 Hz to 16 kHz, −10 to +10 dB in 0.5 dB steps.
  • LoudnessBoosts the bass and treble more as you lower the volume, so quiet sound stays full.
  • PresetsSave equalizer settings by name, switch between them, and export or import them.
  • English and JapaneseThe app's screens are available in both languages.

Before you start

Turn down the volume on your speakers before you open the app and before you quit it.

The app lowers the volume only while it is running. When you quit the app, turn off "Audio processing", or the app crashes, the sound returns to full volume without processing. The volume starts at 100 (the original volume) the first time. Lower it in the popup after opening the app.

Installation

  1. Download SimpleEqualizer-<version>.dmg from Releases and open it.
  2. Drag SimpleEqualizer to the Applications folder.

Opening it for the first time

This app is not notarized by Apple (the author is not a member of Apple's paid Developer Program). macOS blocks it the first time you open it and each time you install a new version. Open it as follows.

  1. Double-click SimpleEqualizer in the Applications folder.
  2. When you see '"SimpleEqualizer.app" Not Opened', click "Done" (do not click "Move to Trash").
  3. Open System Settings and choose "Privacy & Security".
  4. Near the bottom, in the "Security" section, you will see '"SimpleEqualizer.app" was blocked to protect your Mac.' Click "Open Anyway" next to it and enter your Mac password.
  5. If macOS asks again, click "Open Anyway".

Permissions

  • System Audio Recording: needed to process the sound. macOS asks for it the first time you open the app. Allow it.
  • Accessibility: needed to use the keyboard volume keys.

For how to use the popup, the equalizer, presets, and settings, see the README.
